Choose the right category

Publisher / newsletter media

Buy sponsorships, native placements, dedicated sends, or publisher inventory. Buy the placement—not the subscriber file.

Solo ads / dedicated sends

Use as controlled tests. One seller, one creative, one landing page, one source ID, fixed budget, written delivery and refund terms.

Affiliate / performance

Use CPA, revenue share, creator, or publisher distribution when offer-level approval, disclosure, tracking, and suppression are controllable.

Postal / direct mail

Use buyer/subscriber selects, broker access, RFM targeting, and matchback. Keep postal analysis separate from email permission.

Data / modeling / hygiene

Use for records, append, modeling, enrichment, and lawful deployment—not as shortcut permissioned email media.

Discovery / associations

Use to find current owners, publishers, brokers, and contacts. Discovery records are candidates, not approved sources.

Non-negotiable diligence

  • Identify the actual product: placement, dedicated send, data file, lead generation, postal list, or affiliate traffic.
  • Classify the audience: buyer, paid subscriber, free registrant, lead, clicker, modeled, or unknown.
  • Require source, collection path, consent/provenance, sender identity, geography, suppression, and recent delivery/complaint data.
  • Approve offer, claims, From identity, subject, disclosure, creative, landing page, tracking, and frequency.
  • Predefine contribution threshold, complaint ceiling, refund terms, pause rules, make-goods, and evidence access.
  • Never treat a purchased file or “opt-in” claim as automatic permission to send.

Decision rules

Stop immediately

Missing provenance, unclear roles, suppression failure, material complaint/bounce/fraud/refund deviation, unapproved creative, or missing reporting.

Refine

Audience congruence is strong but the offer, creative, landing page, follow-up, or segment needs work.

Scale

Compliance and operations remain sound, repeatable tests meet contribution and quality thresholds, and the next increment has a clear learning objective.

Compound

Route lawful leads/customers and source learning into the first-party house-file system.
