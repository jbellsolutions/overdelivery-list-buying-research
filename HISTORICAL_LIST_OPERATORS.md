## Historical buying lessons

- “Buy around the corner” is not a substitute for source knowledge.
- A broker can be a strategic partner when it knows the offer, the owner, and the test history.
- List rental income can be damaged by careless list usage; short-term response does not equal long-term list value.
- Merge/purge, matchback, and selection history are learning systems, not clerical chores.
