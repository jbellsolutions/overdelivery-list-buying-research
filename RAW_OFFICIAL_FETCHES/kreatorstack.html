Common questions.

Everything you need to know.

What is KreatorStack?

KreatorStack is an AI-powered intelligence platform for the newsletter economy. It matches creators with sponsors, helps brands find the right newsletter audiences, and surfaces newsletter recommendations for readers — powered by a matching engine that learns from every interaction.

How does the AI matching work?

KreatorStack's AI analyzes newsletter content, audience demographics, engagement rates, and historical sponsorship performance. Creators are matched with brands whose products resonate with their specific readers. The more data the platform accumulates, the more precise every match becomes.

Which email platforms do you support?

KreatorStack is fully ESP-agnostic and works with Beehiiv, Substack, Kit, Ghost, Mailchimp, and any platform that gives you access to your subscriber and engagement data.

Is it free to join during beta?

Yes. Creators who join during private beta get full access at no cost, plus priority placement when we launch publicly. Founding creators also shape the product roadmap directly.

When does the platform launch publicly?

We're currently in private beta with a growing cohort of creators. Public launch is planned for later in 2026. Joining now means you'll have an established presence before the broader market opens.
