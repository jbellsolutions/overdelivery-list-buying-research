TECH POWERED LEAD CONVERSION STARTS HERE

THE LEAD-TO-REVENUE GAP IS WIDENING. THIS IS WHERE YOU CLOSE IT.

Generating consumer leads has gotten easier. Converting them hasn’t. Between lead quality issues, handoff breakdowns, attribution gaps, and compliance pressure, the distance between a lead and a closed customer is where revenue is lost — and where most events don’t go. Lead-to-Customer is built for that gap. In Nashville, RevOps leaders, sales ops teams, and senior performance marketers from insurance, home services, mortgage, and financial services come together to work through the conversion systems, AI-powered workflows, and GTM alignment strategies that drive measurable revenue from high-volume consumer lead programs. Single track. Intentionally small. No sessions you’ll skip.

Register now and walk away with frameworks you can deploy as soon as the event ends.

  • Mid-funnel, execution-first content — lead quality, speed-to-contact, AI and automation, attribution, and sales-marketing alignment designed for high-volume B2C conversion programs.
  • Built for revenue owners — CROs, RevOps and sales ops leaders, and demand gen directors responsible for pipeline efficiency and closed revenue, not just lead volume.
  • Peer-driven format — small, single-track, and intentionally curated so the conversations between sessions carry as much weight as the sessions themselves.
  • The experts are in the room — the CMO from 1-800-Plumber + Air. The AVP of Growth from Hugo Insurance. AVP of Marketing from American Collectors Insurance. People running live conversion programs, not consulting on them.

This is not LeadsCon. LeadsCon is top-of-funnel acquisition. This is what happens after the lead comes in.

    The Trust Equation: Privacy, Ethics, and the Future of AI

    Artificial intelligence is now embedded in nearly every stage of the lead-to-customer journey, raising a hard question: how do you scale conversion without eroding the trust that makes conversion possible? From AI-generated content and hyper-personalization to chatbots and voice assistants, lead gen teams are moving fast, and so are the pitfalls: misleading claims, IP exposure, data privacy gaps, and governance that traditional compliance workflows weren't built to catch. This session breaks down how to manage those risks in high-volume consumer lead programs, where a single misstep in consent, disclosure, or automated decisioning can carry outsized legal and reputational risk.

    Attendees will leave with:

    • A practical framework for evaluating AI tools, from content generation to conversational experiences, against emerging privacy and consumer protection standards
    • Clarity on where TCPA, state privacy law, and AI-specific regulation intersect with everyday lead conversion and marketing workflows
    • Strategies for permissioned data use, auditable AI-generated content, and production controls that keep innovation moving without outrunning compliance
    • Guidance on building consent and disclosure practices that hold up to regulatory scrutiny without slowing down speed-to-contact
    • An honest look at where the industry is heading, and how to build trust into your systems before regulators force the issue
