Direct Response Lists

Our list brokerage team can provide access to proven direct mail buyers and responders who have directly purchased or responded to a particular product or service. Let us help you find your next best potential marketing prospects.

LIST OF PROVEN BUYERS FOR YOUR OFFER

As a full service list compiler, broker and aggregator we have access to virtually every mailing list published in the marketplace.

Increase Your Marketing ROI With Known Buyers

When the need to reach a proven buyer is key you will find that a direct response list may be necessary. These lists are comprised of contacts ( both individuals or businesses ) who have previously purchased or inquired about a particular product or service. Typical direct response list sources may include buyers of magazines, membership clubs, catalogs, warranty cards, etc.

Response-generated data lists are available to marketers who are trying to reach individuals and businesses, all of the names on these lists have responded to a specific offer or have purchased a product. Our response lists are typically more targeted since they are based on the behavioral, lifestyle or buying habits of the individuals.

Response List Sources Include:

  • Catalogue Buyers
  • Free Information Inquirers
  • Magazine Subscribers
  • Trade Show Attendees
  • Trade Publication Subscribers
  • Industry Newsletter Subscribers
  • Club Memberships
  • Organization Memberships
  • Product Buyers
  • Current Memberships

*A sample mail piece and list owner approval is often required when renting a subscriptions list.

About us

Amerilist is a leading national provider of targeted mailing, telemarketing, email lists, data processing, data enhancement, printing and advertising services in the U.S and Canada. Our proprietary access to over 60,000 direct marketing databases published by list owners, compilers and list managers is the core of our business model. We segment our operations into four disciplines: list and data sales, data processing, creative design, and distribution. These disciplines include database compilation, list management, list brokerage, data analytics, data hygiene, graphic design, web design and development, digital marketing, email marketing, printing, and mail house/lettershop services amongst others. Our marketing brands include Senior Source List, AWD (Amerilist Web Design), Amerilist Promotional Products and APS Amerilist Print Solutions, Hearing Aid List, Bridal Mailing List and Automotive Mailing List.
