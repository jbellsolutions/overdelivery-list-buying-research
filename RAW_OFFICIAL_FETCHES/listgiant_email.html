How Much Does It Cost to Purchase an Email List?

The cost of purchasing an email list varies depending on its quality, size, and targeting. Here’s a general idea:

  • Price per contact: Email lists typically range from $0.10 to $5 per contact, depending on the specificity and quality.
  • List size: A list of 10,000 emails may cost anywhere from $500 to $10,000, depending on the level of targeting and additional data points provided.
  • Additional features: High-quality lists often include verified emails, job titles, industries, and more, which may increase the cost but enhance the value.
