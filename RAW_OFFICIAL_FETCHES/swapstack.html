Frequently asked questions

How does Swapstack help grow my business?

Email newsletters are one of the most highly engaged and trusted sources of content on the internet. As an advertiser, Swapstack enables you to connect with and sponsor newsletters in multiple ways. The platform enables you to run affiliate deals, connect on a 1:1 basis with publishers, and run scaled sponsorships across hundreds of publications.

How does Swapstack help me monetize my newsletter?

Swapstack is built by creators, for creators. On Swapstack, you can monetize through multiple products: Tip Jars, Affiliate Deals and Sponsorships from advertisers.

How much does Swapstack cost?

Create an account for free! Swapstack's pricing structure is aligned to the value we create. We only make money when you make money! Check out our Pricing page for more details.

Does Swapstack reach my target audience?

With over 900 publishers on the platform, reaching more than 60M readers, our publishers cover dozens of topics and interest areas. Get in touch and we'll help find the right publishers for you to sponsor.

As a publisher, when should I start thinking about monetization?

It is our belief that any publisher of any size can turn their publication into a revenue stream. Our product set allows publishers with 100 subscribers to 100 million subscribers to monetize.
