Premium Shoppable Formats

Shopping Collection

Immersive formats that submerge readers in high-impact shoppable experiences that encourage engagement — whether it be to learn more about the products advertised or to make a purchase. Exclusively offered in Hearst's most premium, largest ad canvases — Super Hero and Mega Hero.

3D Cube Gallery

A gallery display that rotates a four-sided cube, driving curiosity with a unique design to encourage product discovery or shoppability.

Click-to-Cart

Transform high-impact creatives into direct shoppability by automatically adding featured products to a shopping cart at local retailers with a simple click.

Hotspots

Actionable touchpoints layered on creatives to learn more or shop products featured in the ad.

Product Gallery

A gallery that switches frames of featured products with a simplistic, templated design for easy activation that drives to sales.

Shoppable Video

Make premium video assets instantly shoppable with an expandable tray to explore and purchase products.

Store Locator

Drive traffic to your brick and mortar stores with a localized map of nearby locations for consumers to explore and shop products digitally and in store.
