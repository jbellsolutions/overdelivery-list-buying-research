Direct Marketing

SRDS Direct Marketing List Source®

The SRDS Direct Marketing Database gives you sources, costs, and other valuable information to help you find and evaluate more than 65,000 list rental opportunities. Refine your targeted marketing list strategy with these useful datapoints:
  • Uncover direct mail list options across 230 consumer and B2B categories
  • Discover email lists, enhanced lists, high-ticket mail order buyers, new lists, and lists under new management
  • Analyze list universe counts, selects and base rental prices
  • Understand list rental requirements and policies
  • Easily build consideration sets and export data to reports
  • Quickly activate your plans by connecting with list brokers, managers, owners, and compilers
  • Save time using enhanced search, filter, and sort options

SRDS datacards include all the data points you need to create a successful marketing campaign.

  • SRDS category
  • Personnel
  • Summary description
  • List source
  • Selections with counts and rates
  • Other available selections
  • Commission, credit policy
  • Method of addressing
  • Delivery schedule
  • Restrictions
  • Test arrangement
  • Lettershop services
  • Update schedule

Please note: SRDS does not rent mailing lists.
