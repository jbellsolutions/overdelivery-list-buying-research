List Brokerage

The Best in the Business

List brokerage clients seldom leave Conrad Direct. Many have been with us more than 25 years. We keep our eye on the basics:  send the right offer to the right audience and you’ll succeed in direct mail. Conrad Direct’s list brokers have over 200 years of combined experience guiding our clients to direct mail success.

We’ll help you find the right audience – and not using just vertical lists. Today’s leading-edge marketing and fundraising campaigns rely heavily on cooperative databases. We’ve worked in that space since the 1990’s and have refined knowledge of the subtleties and differences among the numerous (and growing number of) databases. We’ll put together the most cost-effective, and most powerful, mix of vertical and database lists to help you achieve your success.

You can count on your Conrad Direct list brokerage team to:

  • Know what’s distinctive about your customers, members, and donors
  • Understand what’s special about your organization – a key selling point to new prospects
  • Expand the universe of lists you can mail to successfully
  • Provide informed consultation regarding reciprocal arrangements and participation in cooperative databases
  • Keep you up-to-date with the latest direct marketing issues and trends
  • Pay strict attention to details and keep costs under control
  • Act with integrity and adhere to the highest ethical standards
List Management

Income and Strategic Leverage

What makes your house file invaluable to you also makes it attractive to other companies and organizations. Some list management clients are focused on maximizing income from list rentals. Others want to use the value of their list for marketing leverage.  Most clients fall somewhere in between. At Conrad, list brokerage and management are integrated.  Every list rental order is reviewed by your Conrad broker to make sure it fits into your overall strategy.

You can count on your Conrad Direct list manager to:

  • Protect your most valuable asset – your customer or donor list
  • Understand your goals and products, and the unique characteristics of the people on your list
  • Overlay your large file with demographic and behavioral enhancements
  • Make money for you - maximize the value of your donors and customers in the list marketplace
  • Pay you promptly
  • Maintain excellent rental and exchange records
  • Keep trade secrets confidential
