Is Your Database Working For You?

If you aren’t currently earning substantial revenue from renting your company’s customer database, then you are missing out on an important opportunity.

Currently, there are ten of thousands of lists available on the rental market, including many provided by some of the largest companies in the world. In today’s competitive economy, companies can’t afford to leave money on the table – especially when it can be earned simply and easily.

How It Works:

Step 1: Direct Marketers are waiting to pay you for the opportunity to call the buyers and inquirers in your company’s customer database. Geon Media Group serves as your agent in finding marketers interested in renting your list. You always maintain ownership of your list, and you keep complete control over the rental process.

Step 2: When a prospect inquires about renting your list, we provide you with the information you need to approve or deny the prospect’s request. Once we receive your approval, we compile the specific data the prospect has requested, collect payment, and deliver the data.

Step 3: Geon Media’s accounting department sends you a check for the rental. It’s that easy! It’s no wonder that so many thousands of companies participate in the process. It’s a simple way to earn significant revenue – for the companies, millions of dollars each year – from business efforts you have already made. If your database isn’t currently performing for you, all you need to do is to give Geon Media Group a call.

We’ll take it from there!
