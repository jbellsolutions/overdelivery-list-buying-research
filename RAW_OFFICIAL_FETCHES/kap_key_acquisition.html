    Home / What We Do

    We believe that accepting responsibility for all aspects of data purchase/placement, processing & enhancement, and segmentation strategy allows us to best influence a positive result. As acquisition experts, our services help alleviate internal capacity issues, streamline the production process, and drive improved results.

    List Brokerage | Straight appeals, Premiums, Sponsorship/continuity programs, Special Event Attendees, Major donor prospects, E-mail lists, International markets

    List Management | Aggressive list promotion, Proactive client representation, Exchange balance management, Cross-category promotion plans, Timely collections and payments

    Data Processing | Merge purge, NCOA, Address hygiene, List fulfillment, List enhancement demographic overlays

    Marketing Intelligence & Analytics | Proprietary analytics tools for performance evaluation, competitive benchmarking, strategic planning, targeting enhancements, and list management reporting

    Further | Display Advertising, Online Lead Generation/Advocacy, Customized Programs, Chaperoned email brokerage, Integrated Campaign Development
