# Final Report — Over Delivery List-Buying Research

**Prepared:** August 1, 2026  
**Framework:** Brian Kurtz / *Over Delivery* direct-response discipline

## Executive conclusion

The strongest acquisition model is not “buy a large email list and blast it.” It is **controlled access to relevant audiences, measured response, first-party capture, and disciplined compounding**.

Use the research as a business process:

1. Define the offer, audience, margin, and success event.
2. Choose the access category that matches the objective.
3. Build a source universe from official evidence.
4. Qualify the source, audience, permission, and operating mechanics.
5. Clear compliance, offer, creative, sender, and suppression controls.
6. Run the smallest useful test with unique tracking and hard stop rules.
7. Reconcile economics, quality, complaints, refunds, and learning.
8. Scale only when the mechanism is repeatable—and route the learning into the owned audience.

**Bottom line:** Buy access, not databases. Test placements, not promises. Capture the response. Build the house file.

## The market map: what each category actually sells

| Category | Real product | Correct business use | Do not assume |
|---|---|---|---|
| Publisher / list owner | Audience relationship and media inventory | Newsletter sponsorship, dedicated send, native placement, direct mail | Subscriber count proves exportable permission |
| Broker / list manager | Selection, access, clearance, scheduling | Build and clear a test universe | Broker representation proves current inventory |
| Newsletter marketplace | Access to publisher placements | Compare and test sponsorship inventory | Marketplace approval proves offer-level clearance |
| Solo-ad provider | A dedicated send from another audience | Controlled response test | Clicks prove quality or consent |
| Affiliate / performance network | Partner distribution, tracking, payouts | CPA, rev-share, creator, and publisher recruitment | Network tracking authorizes every email method |
| Data / audience vendor | Records, modeling, enrichment, hygiene | Direct mail, modeling, append, lawful deployment | A record is automatically permissioned email media |
| Discovery / association | Research and relationship infrastructure | Find owners, publishers, brokers, contacts | A discovery record is an approved media source |
| Tracking / compliance infrastructure | Measurement and operational control | Attribution, suppression, evidence, partner governance | Tracking is audience access |

The critical distinction is between **audience relationship**, **media inventory**, **partner distribution**, **data service**, and **research infrastructure**. They are not interchangeable products.

## Recommended priority order

### 1. Publisher and newsletter media

Start with relevant publisher sales teams and marketplaces such as Paved, beehiiv Ad Network, SmartBrief, Search Engine Journal, Morning Brew, and carefully selected vertical publishers.

Buy the placement—not the subscriber file. Require audience source, geography, paid/free mix, placement, sender identity, delivery and complaint reporting, click definition, make-good terms, suppression handling, and written offer/creative clearance.

### 2. Performance partnerships

Use affiliate and partner systems when the economics can support tracking, customer service, refunds, creative support, and revenue share. Obtain written offer-level approval for email, creative, geography, frequency, disclosures, and suppression.

### 3. Controlled solo-ad tests

Treat Udimi and comparable marketplaces as test environments. Use one seller, one creative, one landing page, one source ID, a fixed budget, a seed/test segment, and written delivery/refund terms. Never scale on clicks alone.

### 4. Postal and data

Use NextMark and broker/manager conversations to discover named buyer/subscriber lists and direct-mail opportunities. Ask for RFM-style selects—recency, frequency, and monetary value. Use data providers for direct mail, modeling, enrichment, and hygiene, not as a shortcut to permissioned email.

### 5. Discovery and relationship development

Use associations, media directories, events, and discovery databases to find the current owner or manager. Treat every discovery record as a candidate until official evidence, current mechanics, and relationship quality are confirmed.

## The operating process

The complete procedure is in [`BUSINESS_PROCESS.md`](BUSINESS_PROCESS.md). Its ten gates are:

1. **Strategy brief** — define product, audience, margin, geography, success event, and first-party capture.
2. **Category selection** — choose publisher media, solo, affiliate, postal, data, discovery, or infrastructure based on the job.
3. **Source universe** — assemble candidates with official URLs and source-level provenance.
4. **Vendor qualification** — score audience truth, congruence, permission clarity, testability, reporting, operations, and relationship quality.
5. **Compliance clearance** — document consent/provenance, roles, sender, suppression, data use, and jurisdiction.
6. **Test design** — fix budget, volume, creative, landing page, tracking ID, attribution, and stop thresholds before launch.
7. **Launch control** — run seed/QA checks, preserve the suppression snapshot, and monitor delivery and reputation.
8. **Measurement** — reconcile sent, delivered, complaints, bounces, unsubscribes, leads, sales, refunds, and contribution.
9. **Scale or stop** — use source/cohort evidence; do not let platform size or seller confidence substitute for results.
10. **Owned-audience compounding** — route lawful leads/customers and learning into the house-file system.

## Minimum diligence before paying

- Identify the legal entity, list owner, broker, actual sender, ESP, advertiser, affiliate, and processor.
- Classify the audience: buyer, paid subscriber, free registrant, lead, clicker, modeled record, or unknown.
- Obtain collection source, signup form, privacy notice, exact consent wording, channel, dates, geography, and sender/brand/category named.
- Reject scraped, harvested, guessed, vague-partner, pre-checked, or unsupported “100% opt-in” claims.
- Require global suppression intake, propagation, audit logs, and a permanent do-not-contact record.
- Approve offer, claims, From identity, subject, disclosure, creative, landing page, tracking, and frequency.
- Contract reporting, invalid-lead/refund rules, complaint/bounce thresholds, pause rights, make-goods, incident notice, and evidence access.

A platform's ability to send, track, or host a record is not proof that the audience can legally or operationally receive a particular offer.

## Test and decision discipline

Every first test gets a test card:

- source/category and cohort;
- offer/creative/landing page;
- budget and volume cap;
- source ID/promo code/phone number;
- primary success event;
- attribution window;
- contribution-margin threshold;
- complaint, bounce, unsubscribe, refund, chargeback, and invalid-lead stop rules;
- owner, measurement window, and next decision.

**Stop immediately** for missing consent evidence, unclear roles, suppression failure, material complaint/bounce/fraud/refund deviation, unapproved creative changes, or missing reporting.

**Refine** when audience congruence is strong but offer, creative, landing page, follow-up, or segment is weak.

**Scale** only after a repeatable test meets contribution and quality thresholds, cohort behavior is understood, controls remain intact, and the next increment has a specific learning objective.

## Compliance boundary

Before email or lead deployment, document signup language, consent source, timestamp, channel, geography, frequency, advertiser disclosure, sender identity, and suppression process. Review CAN-SPAM and applicable state law; review GDPR/UK PECR/ePrivacy where relevant; map CCPA/CPRA sale/share opt-outs; and confirm that the selected ESP permits the proposed source type.

Platform guidance is an operational constraint, not a legal safe harbor. Monitor complaints, authentication, bounces, unsubscribes, invalids, refunds, and source-cohort behavior. This report is an operating guide, not legal advice.

## Final recommendation

Build the business around **permissioned publisher access, performance partnerships, disciplined testing, first-party capture, and source-level learning**. Use postal and data channels where their real product fits. Use discovery tools to build relationships. Keep every source, consent, suppression, result, and decision auditable.

**The asset is not the rented reach. The asset is the owned audience plus the learning system that compounds it.**
