## Best-fit groupings

- **Broad advertiser/publisher networks:** CJ Affiliate, Awin, FlexOffers, Webgains, Tradedoubler, AvantLink.
- **Enterprise partnership-management platforms with partner recruitment:** impact.com, Partnerize, PartnerStack.
- **Media-buying, email or CPA-oriented access:** ClickBank, MaxBounty, Perform[cb], Everflow.
- **Commerce-content and creator monetization:** Skimlinks, Sovrn Commerce, mrge.
- **Network infrastructure rather than an open marketplace:** TUNE and Everflow. They qualify because they support network operators, media buying, publisher login/marketplace workflows or partner recruitment—not merely because they offer tracking.

## Caveats

- Exact **payout timing, payment methods, minimum thresholds and advertiser billing terms** were often behind login or sales workflows. I have not filled those gaps from memory.
- Traffic restrictions are usually **program-specific**. Even where a network supports email, paid media, coupons, influencers or mobile traffic, individual advertisers may prohibit those channels or require pre-approval.
- Rakuten Advertising and Sovrn Commerce were identified from official domains, but their pages were not fully retrievable in this environment; those entries should receive a second verification pass before being treated as fully confirmed.
- No files were created or modified.
