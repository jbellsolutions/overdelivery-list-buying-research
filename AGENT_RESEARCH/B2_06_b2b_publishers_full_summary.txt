### Best initial fits for general offers to business owners, agencies, marketers, and operators

1. **SmartBrief** — strongest explicit newsletter inventory and audience targeting.
2. **Search Engine Journal** — particularly suitable for marketing, SEO, martech, SaaS, and agency offers; explicit dedicated-email and lead-gen products.
3. **B2B Marketing** — strongest fit for B2B agencies, vendors, and professional-services offers.
4. **Chief Marketer** — broad marketer and agency audience across industries.
5. **Marketing Dive** — strong marketer/brand-manager fit, with agency targeting.
6. **Digiday** — strong for marketing, media, adtech, and digital-operator offers.
7. **Industry Dive verticals** — useful when the offer has a specific operator/industry angle rather than a general SMB angle.

**Important limitation:** Most publishers do not publish prices publicly. The current official pages generally require a sales inquiry or media-kit download, so pricing, minimum buys, list segmentation, send dates, and opt-in/compliance details should be confirmed directly before purchase.
