## How these map to Over Delivery principles

### 1. Relationships

Prioritize channels where buyers and suppliers can meet directly:

- **MailCon** — especially useful for email-focused vendor, publisher, deliverability, and data relationships.
- **Affiliate Summit** — broadest likely overlap with solo publishers, affiliates, email operators, networks, and performance media buyers.
- **LeadsCon** — strongest for lead-generation and performance-media relationships.
- **DMAW / ANA** — more structured association networking, committees, supplier introductions, and conferences.
- **Email Innovations World** — smaller, more email-specialized event with explicit networking-app and lead-generation support.

### 2. Testing

Use **NextMark** and **SRDS** to create a source-testing universe rather than selecting one “best” list:

- Filter by category, list type, universe size, selects, pricing, and management status.
- Separate **email**, **postal**, **enhanced**, **high-value buyer**, and **new-under-management** sources.
- Ask each owner/manager for small controlled tests, suppression handling, seed addresses, delivery method, and source-level reporting.
- Track response by exact source, owner/manager, segment, creative, and drop date.

### 3. Source intelligence

Build a source map from:

- **SRDS** for structured list metadata and owner/manager contacts.
- **NextMark** for inventory, rate-card, and provider discovery.
- **MailCon, Affiliate Summit, LeadsCon, and Email Innovations World** exhibitor/sponsor pages for current supplier names and relationship paths.
- **ANA and DMAW member/supplier directories** for association-backed introductions.
- **Outcome Media and Exact Data** as direct broker/provider paths, while keeping them distinct from independent directories.

## Important caveats

- I did **not** find a currently verifiable, publicly accessible “List Owners Association” directory under the commonly suggested domains; I therefore excluded it rather than inventing a current URL.
- `listowners.com` currently resolves to a **HugeDomains domain-sale page**, so it should not be treated as an active list-owner directory.
- `worldata.com` currently redirects to **Outcome Media**; use the current destination rather than the legacy Worldata URL.
- The Email Innovations pages currently expose 2025 event/partner material even though the site remains live. Treat it as a relationship source and verify the next event date before relying on it operationally.
- None of these pages independently proves list quality, consent quality, deliverability, exclusivity, or compliance. Those require owner-level diligence and controlled testing.
