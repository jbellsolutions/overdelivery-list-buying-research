## Corrections to apply to the repository

1. Replace “Worldata” as a current standalone source with **“Worldata URL currently redirects to Outcome Media; verify successor/contracting entity.”**
2. Reclassify **NextMark** as media intelligence/planning and list/media-owner discovery, not raw list delivery.
3. Reclassify **Paved, Swapstack, Udimi** as paid placements/traffic (newsletter sponsorships or solo ads), never “buy a list.”
4. Reclassify **impact.com, PartnerStack, Everflow, Awin, CJ, TUNE, ClickBank, MaxBounty and Perform[cb]** as affiliate/partner/performance platforms or managed acquisition channels—not email-list vendors.
5. Keep **ListGiant** in the list/data-broker category, but downgrade any implied consent/reputation claim: official marketing copy alone cannot establish lawful basis, freshness, deliverability or accuracy.
6. Mark **MaxBounty** as “part of mrge” and **MeritDirect** as “Anteriad” where current-brand status matters.
7. Add a universal rule: a vendor’s marketplace, audience count, approved-partner count, or tracking capability is not evidence that the buyer may export addresses or send unsolicited email.

## Evidence grading used

- **Strong official capability evidence:** live official page accessible and product mechanics are explicit.
- **Mixed:** official raw fetch supports the claim, but live page was blocked, redirected, stale, or materially changed.
- **Weak for quality/reputation:** official marketing claims only; no independent audit, deliverability evidence, complaint data, consent chain, or buyer references were established in this pass.
