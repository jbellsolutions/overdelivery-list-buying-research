## Separation from intermediaries

The list above consists of **media owners or publisher groups**. I did not include broad ad-tech, newsletter-ad marketplaces, programmatic exchanges, or list brokers as candidate list owners. Those can be researched separately, but their presence would not prove access to a publisher’s owned email audience.

## Important qualification

- “Newsletter,” “audience,” or “subscriber” evidence proves a publisher has that channel or audience; it **does not prove third-party list rental, individual-level targeting, or raw-email access**.
- The strongest explicitly newsletter-oriented opportunities are **Morning Brew, The Daily Upside, theSkimm, Hearst, NYT Advertising, and Healthline Media**.
- The strongest niche/enthusiast opportunities are **Outside, FLYING, Farm and Dairy, and NRA Publications**.
- The most direct-response-oriented evidence is from **Healthline Media** (affiliate/digital advertising and budget gate), **FLYING** (qualified leads), **Morning Brew** (native partner campaigns), **theSkimm** (action-oriented audience), and **People Inc.** (consumer action and performance language).
