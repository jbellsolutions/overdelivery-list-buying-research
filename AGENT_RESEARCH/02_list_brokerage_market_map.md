## Public pricing / minimums findings

- **Only NextMark** exposed a usable public price table in the reviewed pages: $159/mo entry shared-minutes plan and $495/mo one-user unlimited plan, up to $3,167/mo for 20 users; custom quotes, prepayment/long-term discounts.
- The brokerage firms generally use count requests, campaign briefs, negotiated CPM/list-owner terms or custom quotes. No reliable public minimum order, CPM, setup fee, list-rental rate card or email deployment minimum was found for KMA, KAP, DRA, WS Ponton, LSC, Amerilist, North American Direct Marketing, McCarthy, or INFOCUS in the pages reviewed.
- DRA’s page visibly references an “Epsilon Price List,” but the actual figures were not available in the captured page; it should not be reported as a current public price sheet without obtaining the document.

## Brian Kurtz / Overdeliver evidence check

- Live exact-phrase searches for `"Brian Kurtz"` with KMA, NextMark, McCarthy Media Group, Wiland, WS Ponton and KAP returned no vendor-specific result; the search for `"Over Delivery" "list brokerage"` likewise produced no relevant vendor connection.
- The official/current search results identify Brian Kurtz’s book **Overdeliver** and his author site, but no reviewed vendor page states an affiliation, endorsement, partnership, employment history or explicit adoption of his principles.
- **Conclusion:** no direct Brian Kurtz/Overdeliver connection is evidenced for the vendors above. This is a negative finding from the reviewed public pages/searches, not proof that no private relationship exists.
- Note: `OverDelivery.com` currently resolves to a domain-for-sale page, so it is not usable as an authoritative current source for a vendor relationship.

## Risk flags for diligence

1. **Permission/compliance:** “Opt-in email” (WS Ponton) and email-list services are capability claims; obtain consent provenance, suppression handling, CAN-SPAM/TCPA/privacy terms and permitted-use language.
2. **Freshness:** Amerilist tested vertical URLs returned 404; INFOCUS footer still shows 2012; Worldata redirected to Outcome Media. Confirm current ownership, inventory and contacts.
3. **Claims vs. auditable inventory:** Counts such as 60,000+ lists/databases and 250M+ records are vendor claims; request current data cards, universe counts, source/recency, match rates and hygiene methodology.
4. **Broker vs. platform vs. agency:** NextMark is primarily a media-information/operations platform; Belardi Wong is a managed cross-channel agency; Salesgenie/Data Axle is a self-service data/marketing platform. They should not be evaluated as interchangeable with a traditional list broker.
5. **Terms not public:** For most firms, pricing, minimums, usage rights, data refresh, cancellation, make-good, seed/suppression and reporting terms require a sales conversation or count request.
