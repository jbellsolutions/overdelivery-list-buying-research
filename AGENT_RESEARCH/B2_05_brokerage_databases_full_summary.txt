## Brian Kurtz / Boardroom lens

### Best fits for classic list intelligence and brokerage

1. **RMI Direct Marketing** — clearest current evidence of actual brokerage + management, datacards, list kits, order/reporting infrastructure, and direct-response orientation.
2. **NextMark** — strongest search/discovery and media-program intelligence analogue; useful for finding potential lists and media programs, but does not itself prove the underlying list-owner relationship or campaign economics.
3. **SRDS** — strongest broad media-planning database; unusually explicit that data is obtained directly from the source and includes rates, demographics, and contacts.
4. **Data Axle** — broad list/data and list-management infrastructure, including proprietary/co-op data and omnichannel activation.
5. **DirectMail.com** — practical list selection plus execution, particularly where the marketer wants one integrated production partner.

### Stronger for modeled audiences, identity, or channel planning than list brokerage

- **Wiland** — valuable for predictive audience construction, congruence, transaction signals, and modeled prospecting.
- **Anteriad** — B2B audience identification, buying groups, content syndication, and cross-channel demand generation.
- **Epsilon** and **Acxiom** — enterprise identity, audience activation, and digital/media infrastructure.
- **Belardi Wong** — strategic customer acquisition and cross-channel media, with explicit triggered direct-mail work, but not a transparent public list-finder database.

### Specific due-diligence questions before testing

For any candidate that survives initial screening, request:

- Current **source/owner relationship** and whether the list is owner-managed, brokered, cooperative, modeled, or compiled.
- Universe size, recency, update cadence, and whether counts are **available names**, gross universe, or modeled reach.
- Historical **test results by offer/category**, including continuation history where available.
- Selectivity and congruence variables: purchase behavior, donor/customer recency, category, source title, geography, demographics, and channel permissions.
- NCOA/CASS/duplicate/suppression treatment for postal files.
- Email permission basis, opt-out handling, phone consent status, and whether digital activation is deterministic, modeled, or probabilistic.
- Whether the provider permits a controlled **seeded test**, split-cell test, or source-level reporting.
- Pricing differences between a direct owner relationship, brokered rental, managed service, and modeled audience activation.

## Issues encountered

- The rtrvr research endpoint was unavailable/time-outed, so I used live official site retrieval through browser/HTTP requests instead.
- Several legacy or less-certain domains were not included because they were parked, DNS-unavailable, or failed certificate checks rather than showing current official capability.
- **ListSource** is explicitly in transition to CoreLogic’s Araya platform; treat it as a current-but-retiring product rather than a stable long-term recommendation.
- **MeritDirect** is not reported as an independent current vendor because its official URL redirected to **Anteriad**.
