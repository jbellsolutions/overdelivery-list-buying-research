## Current versus historical status

- All candidates above had live official pages, current navigation, active contact/quote/request paths, or current service descriptions during this review.
- The strongest evidence of traditional direct-response list brokerage/list management is from **NextMark, Names in the News, MEGA, InFocus Marketing, W.S. Ponton, DirectMail.com, List Solutions, AmeriList, Arleen Smith Marketing, Prospects Influential, Dataman Group, and Dynamicard**.
- Some providers also sell data, fulfillment, email, modeling or digital services. They are included only where their current official material specifically identifies postal/direct-mail lists, list rental, list brokerage, list management, or prospect-mailing services.

## Coverage gap

Public web evidence is much stronger for broad consumer prospect lists, nonprofit donor lists, specialty lists and list marketplaces than for private catalog-owner files, magazine subscriber files, proprietary buyer files and individual list-manager rosters. Many traditional list owners expose only a data card or broker contact rather than a public inventory. A production repository should therefore treat this as a broker-discovery layer and request current data cards, universe counts, pricing, recency, usage restrictions and availability directly before purchasing.
