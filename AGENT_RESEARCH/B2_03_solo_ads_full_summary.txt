### Highest-priority shortlist

1. **Udimi** — best starting point for marketplace-based comparison, seller ratings, transaction intermediation, and refund workflow.
2. **TrafficForMe** — credible-looking managed option when the buyer wants the vendor to match the offer to a list and deliver quickly; require independent tracking.
3. **AffiliateSoloAds** — attractive test pricing and unusually explicit delivery/refund distinction, but downgrade confidence because the legal Terms page is visibly unfinished.
4. **SoloAdsX** — useful directory and reputation-research layer, not a protected marketplace.
5. **Edgars Solo Ads** — only as a controlled test after accepting the no-refund policy and validating traffic independently.

## Important discipline conclusions

- **None of these services sells list ownership.** They rent access to a vendor’s audience for a dedicated email send. The buyer receives clicks/traffic, not subscriber records or continuing permission to email the audience.
- **Do not confuse this with affiliate traffic generally.** A solo ad is specifically an email/list-owner send; vendors may support affiliate links, CPA offers, or funnels, but that does not make the traffic source owned media.
- **Buy clicks, not promises.** A defensible order should define unique-click counting, bot/duplicate handling, minimum geography, delivery window, tracking URL, make-good/refund terms, and whether the vendor writes the email.
- **Use an independent tracker and a unique landing page.** Vendor-reported clicks alone are insufficient. Compare tracker clicks, analytics sessions, opt-ins, downstream sales, geography, time distribution, and repeat-user anomalies.
- **Start with a small, capped test.** Do not infer list quality from a vendor’s claimed subscriber count, testimonials, or “Tier-1” label.
- **No vendor reviewed publicly guarantees conversions.** AffiliateSoloAds and Edgars explicitly disclaim sales/lead guarantees; TrafficForMe guarantees traffic quality/delivery rather than revenue. That is the correct distinction.
- **Compliance remains unverified.** The reviewed pages generally do not provide enough evidence to independently confirm consent provenance, suppression handling, GDPR/UK PECR compliance, or CAN-SPAM operational controls. Treat “opt-in list” claims as unproven until the vendor answers those questions.

## Negative / excluded findings

- **Safe-Swaps:** The domain currently redirected to Udimi during live testing, so it should not be treated as an independent current marketplace.
- **Clickonomy:** The `www` endpoint returned a 403 Elasticsearch authorization error, while the root domain did not resolve reliably. I found no usable current marketplace evidence.
- **soloads.com:** Redirected to a parked/domain-sale page rather than an operating solo-ad marketplace.
- **Fiverr:** It can expose inexpensive traffic sellers, but it is a general gig marketplace with no solo-ad-specific list provenance or standardized traffic-quality controls. It is not aligned with disciplined list-buying unless the individual seller can provide unusually strong evidence.

No local files were created or modified. The main issue encountered was Cloudflare blocking direct Udimi pages; official Udimi help URLs and mechanics were therefore corroborated through indexed official-page results rather than a direct page scrape.
