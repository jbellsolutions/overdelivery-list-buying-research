# Affiliate networks and affiliate-management companies

**Research checked:** 2026-08-01. First-party pages were preferred. A few policy/pricing pages were moved, JavaScript-rendered, or blocked; those gaps are marked as unknown rather than inferred permission.

## Executive takeaways

- **Best fit for a publisher/email-list owner:** ClickBank (explicitly markets to email marketers and supports CPA/revenue-share), Awin/CJ/impact.com for mainstream brand inventory, and specialist lead-gen networks only after written approval for the exact email traffic source.
- **Best fit for B2B SaaS:** PartnerStack (publisher/affiliate marketplace plus partner-program software), with Everflow or TUNE when the advertiser wants to operate its own network or multi-channel tracking.
- **Best fit when the advertiser needs recruiting and hands-on management:** Acceleration Partners or Gen3 Marketing; these are agencies, not open affiliate marketplaces, and pricing is quote-based.
- **Email is never “automatically approved” merely because a platform supports affiliate links.** Obtain written offer-level approval, retain proof of opt-in/consent, identify the sender and advertiser, and confirm suppression/unsubscribe and creative rules before sending.

## Practical email-list-owner approval checklist

1. **Source and consent:** publisher can document how recipients opted in, the date/source, geography, purpose and advertiser/brand disclosure.
2. **Offer-level approval:** written approval from the advertiser/network account manager naming the exact offer, domains/lists, countries, send frequency and allowed creative.
3. **Compliance controls:** clear sender identity, physical address where required, one-click/unsubscribe, suppression handling, complaint/bounce monitoring, and no purchased/scraped lists.
4. **Traffic taxonomy:** distinguish newsletter/email, solo ads, co-registration, SMS, push, incentivized, paid search, social and sub-affiliate traffic; approval for one does not imply approval for the others.
5. **Claims and verticals:** finance, health and education require extra review of claims, disclosures, licensing/geography and landing-page language; health offers deserve the strictest substantiation review.
6. **Attribution and payment:** confirm cookie/server-to-server tracking, deduplication, validation/hold periods, reversal rules, caps, payout minimums, tax forms and who bears chargebacks/fraud.
7. **Written audit trail:** save the current program terms, email approval, creative approval, suppression evidence and reports before sending.

## Exclusions

Excluded from the shortlist: adult/pornography, gambling/betting, illegal drugs or unlicensed pharmaceutical sales, deceptive “make money fast”/fake investment schemes, piracy/counterfeit goods, and unsolicited or purchased-list bulk-email vendors. A general-purpose network may still carry a prohibited offer; that is why every offer requires current program-level review.

## Source notes and limitations

- CJ publisher page was live at `https://www.cj.com/publisher`; the older `/publishers` and `/terms-of-service` paths redirected/moved during checking.
- Awin’s Code of Conduct resolved to a current PDF; the browser could display it but not expose all PDF text in the accessibility tree.
- ClickBank’s older Help Center email-policy URL returned “page doesn’t exist”; the current affiliate page explicitly lists email marketers, but that is not a substitute for the missing policy.
- Gen3 and Acceleration Partners were accessible in the browser, while direct terminal fetches returned Cloudflare 403; their live homepages remain the current evidence URLs.
- Retriever cloud scraping was unavailable during this run, so no claims rely on its output.
