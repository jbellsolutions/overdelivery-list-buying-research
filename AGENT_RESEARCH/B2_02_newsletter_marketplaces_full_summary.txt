## Research completed

I reviewed public, current official pages and separated:

- **Newsletter sponsorships:** an ad/native placement inside an editorial newsletter, usually sold by issue, slot, CPM/CPC, or campaign.
- **Dedicated email / solo ads:** the buyer supplies the offer and a list owner sends a standalone email to its audience. This is generally higher-risk and less editorially integrated.
- **Adjacent tools:** sponsor discovery databases or creator-deal platforms that may facilitate introductions but do not necessarily sell inventory directly.

Research date: **August 1, 2026**.

## Practical classification

### Best fits for buying newsletter placements

1. **Paved** — strongest large-scale marketplace/network option.
2. **beehiiv Ad Network** — strong native network integration, but advertiser pricing is sales/platform controlled.
3. **LetterWell** — clearest self-serve marketplace and escrow mechanics.
4. **Hecto** — transparent platform fee and creator-facing marketplace workflow.
5. **Passionfroot** — useful where newsletter sponsorship is part of a broader creator/media campaign.

### Best fits for discovering sponsors rather than directly buying inventory

- **SponsorGap**
- **KreatorStack**
- **Newsletter Sponsorship Brand Database**

### Dedicated-send / solo-ad category

- **Udimi**
- **TrafficForMe**
- **Solo Ads X**

These should be evaluated separately because the advertiser’s message may be sent as the entire email, often to a list whose consent, segmentation, and complaint history are less transparent than a mainstream newsletter sponsorship.

## Main findings and caveats

- The market is split between **premium newsletter sponsorship networks** and **small creator marketplaces**. The former are usually sales-led or platform-integrated; the latter expose more creator-level pricing and inventory.
- **Public pricing is uncommon** for large networks. The clearest published pricing found was Hecto’s **5% fee**, SponsorGap’s **$39/$89/$199 monthly plans**, and TrafficForMe’s **$59/$75/$299 starting prices**.
- Many “marketplaces” are actually **sponsor discovery or outreach products**, not guaranteed inventory exchanges.
- Reach claims such as “thousands of newsletters,” “millions of readers,” and “25k+ deals” are labeled as **unverified marketing metrics** unless supported by independently inspectable data.
- For any dedicated-send purchase, require documentation of: list acquisition and consent, unsubscribe handling, suppression against prior complaints, audience geography, historical delivery metrics, and refund/replacement rules.

## Issues encountered

- **Paved, LiveIntent, Jeeng, and Udimi** used Cloudflare or bot protection during direct browser retrieval. Paved’s public homepage was still retrievable via a normal HTTP request; LiveIntent, Jeeng, and Udimi were therefore assigned lower confidence for details that could not be checked directly.
- **GoLetter** was reviewed but not included as a core candidate because its public material emphasizes newsletter growth/directory services and does not clearly demonstrate a current advertiser booking marketplace.
