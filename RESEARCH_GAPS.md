## Universal boundary

No publisher audience, newsletter subscriber count, marketplace listing, approved-partner count, tracking capability, or database size proves that the buyer may export addresses or send unsolicited email. Promote a source to a live test only after current media terms, consent/provenance, sender responsibility, suppression, and complaint/remediation controls are documented.
